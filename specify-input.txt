/speckit.specify Build a proof of concept for Agri-GIS Intelligence that combines QGIS and Dify to support field-level pest and disease diagnosis, pesticide spray calculation, and field management.

The system must help users understand the condition of a selected field and produce practical guidance that is grounded in spatial data, field attributes, and uploaded crop photos. The goal is not to replace map visualization, but to add a decision layer that combines field context, image evidence, and deterministic calculation.

The proof of concept will use QGIS to manage field polygons and export a field summary CSV, then use a web app to load the selected field into an `agri_context` JSON payload. The system may use public or project-provided data such as field polygons, crop type, soil information, field history, and optionally nearby environmental context. Dify will be responsible for orchestrating diagnosis language, retrieving label knowledge, and returning grounded explanations, while a code step must perform the spray calculation.

The system must support a multimodal workflow with:
- a selected field polygon and its attributes
- one or more uploaded crop symptom images
- a knowledge base of pesticide label data such as dilution ratio and spray volume per 10a
- a deterministic calculation step for total spray volume and pesticide amount

The system must support questions and tasks such as:
- What kind of field condition is this image and context consistent with?
- What should be checked next in this field?
- How much spray liquid is needed for this field?
- How much pesticide is needed based on the selected product label?
- Does this field history suggest any extra caution?

The system must return:
- a short diagnosis-oriented summary
- a short practical next-step summary
- calculated spray volume and pesticide amount when a product label is available
- explicit grounds for each answer using the selected field context, image evidence, and label data

The system must avoid unsupported claims. It must not make hard assertions about disease name, severity, safety, or treatment outcome unless directly supported by the uploaded image and available data. Any calculation must be traceable and must not be left to free-form LLM arithmetic.

Success means users can select a field, upload a crop image, and receive a practical response that combines field context with an exact spray calculation, while every answer includes interpretable reasons tied to the source data and label information.
